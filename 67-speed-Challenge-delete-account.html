67 Game - Speed Challenge — Delete Account

Effective date: September 2, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your 67 Game - Speed Challenge (also called 67) account and the data associated with it.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to shish.hamish@gmail.com from the same email address you used to create the account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my 67 Game account.
  3. Include the account email in the message so we can identify the account.

We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days of receiving a valid request.

Data that is deleted

When we process your request, we delete:

  • Your Firebase account (email and password credentials)
  • Display name
  • Email stored with the account
  • High scores and leaderboard entry
  • Push notification tokens
  • 1v1 / duel battle records tied to your account

Data that is kept or not deleted by this request

  • Data stored only on your device (local avatar, settings, and guest play data): uninstall the app to remove it.
  • Advertising data processed by Google AdMob is handled by Google, not by this deletion request.
  • Data we are legally required to keep, if any, will be retained only for as long as required by law.

Guest players

If you play as a guest, you do not have an account. Uninstalling 67 Game - Speed Challenge from your device removes guest data stored on that device.

Contact

Account deletion requests: shish.hamish@gmail.com
